STANDARDS & VERIFICATION CRITERIA

Community Guidelines

How we maintain high signal, honest data, and zero noise across the EarnRadar directory.

1. The Verification-First Standard

EarnRadar exists to separate real, reproducible earning methods from exaggerated marketing claims. Every method submitted to the radar is evaluated by human moderators against strict reproducibility standards.

2. What Makes a High-Quality Method Submission

  • Actionable Step-by-Step Instructions: Clear prerequisites, account creation workflows, tools needed, and day-to-day execution.
  • Realistic Time & Skill Estimates: Honest representation of learning curve (e.g. 5 hours of setup vs. immediate start).
  • Zero Hidden Costs: Explicit disclosure of software subscriptions, gas fees, or capital required.
  • Transparent Risk Assessment: Highlighting saturation risks, account banning risks, and chargeback possibilities.

3. Evidence Standards

When providing proof of earnings or operational status:

  • Screenshots must clearly show platform interfaces, transaction dates, and valid payout statuses (e.g. Stripe, PayPal, Upwork, Steam, AdSense).
  • Redaction Mandatory: Never include real bank routing numbers, credit card digits, national identification numbers, or residential addresses.
  • Doctored images (inspected via metadata and image forensics) result in immediate, permanent account bans.

4. Prohibited Content

  • No affiliate links that bypass transparent disclosures.
  • No gambling, sports betting, or unregistered binary options schemes.
  • No paid review manipulation or coordinated upvoting rings.
  • No multi-level marketing (MLM) or "pay to join and recruit friends" models.

5. Respectful Collaboration & Chat

Treat fellow members with respect. Any harassment, unsolicited spam, extortion, or fraudulent propositions will be met with immediate expulsion and IP blacklisting.
