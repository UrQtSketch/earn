LEGAL & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Effective immediately

1. Overview and Commitment

EarnRadar ("we", "our", or "us") operates as a community-driven verification platform for online earning methods. We take data privacy seriously and adhere strictly to principles of data minimization, transparency, and user privacy protection.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum required information necessary to provide our discovery, verification, and collaboration services:

  • Account Credentials: Email address, encrypted passwords, and profile usernames.
  • Profile Preferences: Optional bios, skills, countries of origin, and notification settings.
  • Community Contributions: Method submissions, step-by-step instructions, comments, earnings proof screenshots, and video demonstration links.
  • Direct Communications: Messages exchanged through our internal messaging system.

3. Evidence & Financial Data Protection

When uploading proof screenshots (such as payout receipts or dashboard confirmations), users are explicitly instructed to redact personally identifiable financial information (bank account numbers, physical addresses, national ID numbers). We do not store unredacted bank account credentials.

4. Use of Data

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Authenticate your session securely via email OTP.
  • Moderate and verify submitted earning opportunities.
  • Facilitate secure peer-to-peer collaboration requests.
  • Prevent fraud, abusive behavior, spam, and financial scams.

5. Zero Data Brokering

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data, contact information, or financial activity to third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

6. Security Measures

All passwords are cryptographically hashed using Argon2/Bcrypt algorithms. All network traffic is encrypted via TLS/HTTPS, and authentication tokens are secured with HttpOnly, SameSite cookies.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to inspect, export, or permanently delete your account and associated submission data at any time from your account settings or by contacting support@earnradar.io.
